A warlock whose patron is [[Zyblina]]. She was trying to the attention of [[Mister Witch]] and [[Mister Light]] during the [[Witchlight Carnival]] and caused an elephant stampede during the Bigtop Extravaganza. She also upset a dragonfly at the dragonfly riding attraction, stole [[Candlefoot]]'s voice, and used his voice and form to heckle [[Halisha]] the mermaid while she was performing.
