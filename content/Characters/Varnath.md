### Notes

A human woman with black hair and not a lot of patience. Often found in the library at Dragon's Rest, she keeps an eye out for zombies and shipwrecks. When the party first meets her, she explains that she had been investigating the frequent shipwrecks with fellow Dragon's Rest resident [[Rix]]. The problem is she hasn't seen Rix in over 5 days. She's getting worried about him...
