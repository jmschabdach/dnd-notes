### Background

Name: Alexa Heartwood

Physical description: Alexa is a medium sized person of human appearance with the exception of her Aasimir traits: metallic gold freckles on her face, metallic gold highlights in her amber eyes, and metallic copper hair normally pulled back into a braid. 

Q: Where did you come from?
A: Alexa is from a farm outside of Neverwinter.

Q: Who were you raised by?
A: Alexa was raised by her mom until she died, then lived with her aunt, uncle, and cousins. 

Q: Have you overcome any difficulties or challenges to get where you are today?
A: Alexa's mom died when she was younger and she never knew her dad. Before her mom passed away, she told Alexa that one day she would be ready to learn more about herself and explore the world. She would know when it was the right time.

Q: What motivates you to adventure?
A: She's excited to explore the world, but her top priority is finding out about herself and her family. 

Q: What have you done that you're particularly proud of?
A: Alexa got into a lot of fights with other kids. The town blacksmith took pity on her and taught her how to use a sword. She practiced a lot outside of farm work. Now she consistently beats the kids who used to pick on her.

Q: What do you regret in life so far?
A: Alexa's biggest regret was that she couldn't save her mom. There wasn't anything she could have done, but she still feels bad about it.

Q: Other personality traits?
A: She loves the color green and hates pickled beets. And mushrooms. Especially after the myconid caves on Stormwreck Isle.

Q: Why are you getting on the boat to Shipwreck Isle?
A: She had a dream from her mom telling her that it was time to go learn about herself and Shipwreck Isle was the first place she needed to go to?
