Name: Alexa
Physical description: Alexa is a 12 year old girl who looks like any other 12 year old human girl in her hometown. I have dark brown hair, eyes the color of brown bottles (Momma calls them amber), and a ton of freckles across my cheeks (Momma calls them angel kisses). They're different from other freckles though - the ones on my cheeks remind me of stars and Annabeth from town's freckles are just plain brown. But she gets lots in the summer and I don't, my skin just gets more tan, so it works out. I'm tall enough that I can reach the second shelf in the kitchen cabinet when I stand on tiptoe and really stretch. Momma yells at me when she sees me do that, she says I'll drop something by accident and it will break.

Where did you come from?
I live in Farmington, well ok an hours walk outside of Farmington, on our farm. We can get into town faster when we're allowed to borrow Nan and Ol' Stinker, the gray horse and the patchy brown horse, to pull the cart but they're usually needed in the fields.

Who were you raised by?
Well me and Momma live with Uncle Stephan and Aunt Lucy and the all the Heartwoods cousins: Tiran, Lillian, Blake, and the twins Mikel and Camile. Tiran is going to leave soon though, he's marrying the shopkeeper's daughter who lives next door to Annabeth. I'm going to miss him. Don't tell the others but I think he's my favorite. He used to give me piggyback rides and always let me practice fighting with him and Blake and the rest. Now he lets me sit quiet with him and he doesn't make me feel weird about my freckles or for the things I say and do that Mikel and Camile laught at me for. He's nice. Lillian is also nice. She doesn't like to fight but she helped me learn how to sew so Momma doesn't get mad when my breaches get holes in the knees. But it's not my fault they do! When we're not doing field work I like to explore the woods way out back and somehow they always put new tears in my clothes. Momma says Mikel and Camile aren't trying to be mean when they laugh at me, I'm just an odd bird and there's nothing wrong with that but not everyone's going to understand. She never laughs at me. There's more cousins and aunts and uncles in town but these are the ones me and Momma live with.

Have you overcome any difficulties or challenges to get where you are today?
I'm used to the other kids in town making fun of me. Sometimes it feels like I can hear their conversations around corners. They don't like when I join in right in the middle so I learned to keep that quiet. Sometimes I also feel like I could reach out and touch things that are real far away. The barn cat had kittens last spring and I swear I could hear them mewling from the house and feel their warm little fuzzy bodies from across the barn. Momma didn't like it when I said that. She told me to keep nonsense like that to myself. But Tiran listened to me. He said whatever let me hear and feel those things was a gift. 

Then last year we had a fire in one of the sheds one night. I woke up screaming because it I couldn't breathe through the smoke even though I wasn't anywhere near it. Momma was about to tell me to settle down when we saw the firelight through the window. We all rushed outside with water to fight it and we saved half the shed and all the animal feed in it. Uncle Stephan said it must have been a stray lightning strike but I think Aunt Lucy thinks it's my fault. 

The bad thing about the fire is right after that Momma got sick. She coughs a lot and sometimes she doesn't have enough energy to leave the house or even get out of bed. She started sleeping a lot more recently. I try to take care of her, but nothing seems to help. She's just tired.

I think that's why Momma wanted me to come to the temples in Neverwinter. Uncle Stephan and Tiran were planning to try to get some medicine and Momma told them I should go too. There was something about finding the right person to help and how they would help me with whatever came next. I hope I find that person but so far no luck. Uncle Stephan and Tiran have to go back to the farm tomorrow. 

What motivates you to adventure?
Tiran showed me a map in a book one time and pointed out where Farmington is. It was such a little dot! When I asked where the farm was, he said the dot that was Farmington was on top of the farm, too. I want to see all the places on that map and then go home and tell Momma about them.

But first I have to learn about myself. 

What have you done that you are particularly proud of?
It look a long time, but now I can beat Mikel and Blake each when we swordfight! We don't have real swords, but we made play ones. I practiced a lot with Tiran and watched him and Blake fight for months before they let me try. Ooh, and I can run real fast, want to see?

What do you regret in life so far?
I regret not saying bye to Annabeth before I came to Neverwinter. I didn't realize it was such a long way away. And I regret putting that snakeskin in Mikel's bed. It was mean, and he dropped a rotten egg on me for it. I don't regret leaving Norbert with Momma. He's my owlbear toy that keeps me safe when I feel scared. He can keep her safe for a while until I get back.

Any other personality details you'd like to share?
My favorite color is green, like the color of new spring plants. I wish I knew where my angel kiss freckles came from. I absolutely hate pickled beets. They're a pretty color but they don't taste as nice as they look.

Why are you getting on the boat to Shipwreck Isle?
Tiran mentioned something about a boat to an island with a temple and they might be able to help me there. He's going to help me find it tomorrow before he and Uncle Stephan leave.
