- [[Runara]] is tight lipped. She says that [[Sparkrender]] seems to be trying to suck magic out of a sorceror's tower on the south side of the island. (From [[Session 03]])
- After several adventures on other parts of the island, [[Runara]] asks the party to go to the sorceror's tower in search of her student [[Adron]]. She and [[Adron]] had had a fight and she hasn't seen him since. She thinks he might be there.
- On the way there, the party sees a dragon flying overhead a few times.
- When we get there, we try to be sneaky for a bit, but eventually [[Norton Rooftrellen|Norton]] and [[Alexa Heartwood|Alexa]] each fail at lifting a drawbridge, drawing [[Sparkrender]]'s attention.
- A grueling battle ensues. 
    - Sparkrender gets his lightning breath weapon back every turn.
    - [[Norton Rooftrellen|Norton]] and [[Alexa Heartwood|Alexa]] are immediately downed
    - [[Five Dollar Foot Long|Five Dollar]] helps [[Drok Thornsaga|Drok]] repeated get attacks with advantage against [[Sparkrender]]
    - The wyrmling finally dies with a graze to the carotid artery.
- The party finds [[Adron]] chained up the floor below Sparkrender's hoard. 
- We free [[Adron]] and he returns to [[Dragon's Rest]]
