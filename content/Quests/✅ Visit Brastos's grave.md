The packet recovered from the Compass Rose contains a logbook from a member of the crew. It details a prayer by Alethea, the previous captain of the ship. She made it over the braided hair talisman: she wants to be united with her husband no matter their end. The god of the dead seems to have granted her prayer - she and the rest of her crew are coming back as zombies.

Alethea's husband, Brastos, died 80 years ago and is buried atop the hill on the island near the shipwreck. We should go visit his grave.
