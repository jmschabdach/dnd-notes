Mithral is a light, flexible metal. If the armor normally imposes disadvantage on Dexterity ([Stealth](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills)) checks or has a Strength requirement, the mithral version of the armor doesn't.

This armor is made of narrow vertical strips of metal riveted to a backing of leather that is worn over cloth padding. Flexible chain mail protects the joints.

  

AC 17, no dex bonus
