### Notes
Late middle aged man living in Dragon's Rest who harvests mushrooms and makes potions. A beast is preventing him from communicating with the local myconid colony located on the south side of the island (see map for location). He gives the party a sack of decaying food scraps to give to the myconids as a sign of goodwill.

When the party returns from the myconid caves for the first time, they claim Tarack asked them to kill the fungal octopus that was blocking him from the myconid colony.
