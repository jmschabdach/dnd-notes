[[Rix]] the kobold is missing! He usually works with [[Varnath]] and helps investigate shipwrecks. Approximately once a week, a new ship wrecks. The first shipwreck, the Compass Rose, is at the northernmost island of the archipelago north of Dragon's Rest. [[Rix]] has gone missing before but not for this long (5 days).

We found [[Rix]]! He was in a bag being attacked by Kobolds. We saved him and he made it back safely to Dragon's Rest.
