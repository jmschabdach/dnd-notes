In preparation for the coup, we discuss what to do when things go down, give up, and get in a boat. Mara convinces [[Illig|King Illig]] to free [[Margot]]. He agrees under the condition she helps rebuild the balloon factory she lit on fire. We go to free her but there's a loud bang from an area we passed the day before. We deploy [[Sir Voolp]] to go free [[Margot]] while we investigate the bang. 

It takes 30 minutes to navigate through the fog since we accidentally let [[Glurk]] steer, but after Mara takes over we arrive at the site where there used to be a balloon. The bullywugs there say a female bullywug who looked a lot like [[Margot]] stole it. She took it north. We go check the holding cell and [[Sir Voolp]] confirms that she was gone once he arrived.

> Find Sir Gullibex at once. The Revolution lives!

Mara convinces [[Bix]] and [[Glurk]] to at least investigate the area where the last revolution was located.

There's a cauldron sitting on a bed of coals. Mara tries to touch it with Mage Hand but as soon as the hand touches it, it runs away. A little guy pops out of the coals. He asks if we want to get inside the cauldron and tells us that we need to know the password. He will tell us what's inside and the password if we bring him a bundle of dried wood. We agree to those terms.

Since we're nearby, [[Bix]] decides to go claim his stead. The guard who was previously there is in the toad's mouth and gets swallowed and digested. [[Bix]] manages to get onto the toad, which then runs out of the house it was being stabled in with [[Bix]] on top. The toad jumps into the water and eventually throws him off. [[Bix]] swims to shore and [[Glurk]] tries to jump on the toad. [[Glurk]] misses but tries to push it to shore anyway. Farther into the water, the toad gets attacked and killed by a Marrow. It turns into stone which pisses off the Marrow, so it goes to attack Glurk. 

We decide to go see [[Bavlorna]]. At the top of the wooden door we hear [[Elmer]]'s wolf howling. We knock on the locked door several times and announce why we're there. Finally we convince [[Glurk]] to break down the door.

Inside is room with a pool that's 1 foot deep. In the middle of the pool is the top of a well with stinky water. At one end of the pool is a dirty mirror and at the other is a floating lily pad. There is a wooden statue of a wolf on a shelf near the door. In the far corner are 4 large dressing dummies and 3 smaller dressing dummies. 

Bix walks up to a large dummy that's bare except the hat and takes the hat. The hat is not a hat.
