### Summary
We wandered around this new realm. Bix and Mara got confirmation that their lost items were on this plane. The party intimidated a group of the [[Rabbit Mafia]] into not fighting them, then tried to rescue a stranded [[Sir Talavar]] from a crashed air balloon basket. Before we could, he was yoinked through a window in the tower where the balloon landed. The parted did some light breaking and entering, animal abuse, and arson to the tower before emerging with [[Sir Talavar]] in tow. After traveling some distance from the distressed ettercap trying to dowse the flames threatening to consume his home, the part found a safe place to make camp and set about trying to free [[Sir Talavar]] while quizzing him for information about this new realm that turned out to be [[Hither]].
