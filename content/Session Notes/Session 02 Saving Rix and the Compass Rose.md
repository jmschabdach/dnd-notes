Upon waking, [[Norton Rooftrellen]] realizes that a few of Alexa's silver freckles disappeared. She used to have 34, now she has 30.

- Party takes the rowboat, [[Drok Thornsaga|Drok]] navigating
- See an island with smoke rising
- Party goes to smoke island, hears kobolds yelling at something on the ground. In case it's Rix, the party sneaks up
- Alexa steps on a branch alerting the kobolds. They attack
	- ~~Kobold 1: (12) 8~~
	- ~~Kobold 2: (13) 6~~
	- Kobold 3: (23) 7
	- Kobold 4
	- Kobold in a sack - likely not an ad
	- Winged Kobold: (18) 6
- [[Alexa Heartwood|Alexa]] helps the guy out of a sack while [[Drok Thornsaga|Drok]] and [[Norton Rooftrellen]] loot the bodies. Norton gets an amulet that when attuned allows 1 cast of dissonant whisper
- The other kobolds were followers of Sparkrender, a young dragon, who hate the followers of Renara due to the feud of the chromatic dragons vs the metallic dragons
- It's Rix! He was looking at the Compass Rose and got attacked. We short rest with Rix until he gets his breath back.
- "Being brave can hurt"
- Looking around the island, this was a hastily assembled camp. We can't find out any extra info
- We continue to the Compass Rose: 
	- Climb up to main deck -- option we picked
	- Hole in hull underwater
- Five Dollar Foot Long finds shiny things in the crows nest. He picks up a rusty iron helmet, but drops it. [[Norton Rooftrellen|Norton]] catches it, phew.
- Foredeck and rear deck have broken masts, kinda weird. Norton goes up to the rear deck and investigates the wheel. It snaps off its axle.
- Counterclockwise from the bottom right, 
	- Room with a table, probably a mess hall
	- 6 double bunks
	- (Rustling and moaning behind a closed door)
	- Galley with a skeleton. The crabs on it make it look like it's moving
- We decide to open the door with the sounds. The door is stuck. Drok is not strong. Norton eldritch blasts the door open. There are 2 zombie sailors in there
	- Zombie 1: (11, 13) 7+3+5+4+9
	- Zombie 2: (23) 13+11
- Alexa has a bloody nose. At Drok's suggestion, she takes some of the dirt on his head and rubs it on her face
- Loot the room
	- Desk: pouch with 50G, plus a set of cartographer's tools and a dagger
	- Bookshelf: disintigrating books and scrolls
	- The hole: seawater filled lower deck. There's another hole below, like something heavy fell through.
- Drok and Alexa take the stairs. There's a zombie.
- Norton jumps down the hole. A large zombie wanders out of the shadows and gets a free attack on him.
	- Ghoul: (12) 9+9+4
	- Zombie: (7, 9) 8+4+5
- Loot the room
	- 10 1lb bars of silver
	- 2 bone candlestick holders carved to look like dragons (worth 25G)
- Looking down the hole, we see a large iron chest. No baddies.
- Norton flexes on us and brings the magic chest up.
- The chest is completely dry inside. It contains a pouch and a pair of boots, plus a wax-paper wrapped packet. The boots and the packet are magic.
- Alexa takes the pouch and leads the way upstairs with Norton and Drok following. A bird creature is perched on top of the crow's nest
	- Bird (harpy): (7, 15) 14+7+4+10
- Investigate the room with
	- The beds: nothing of value in personal effects, but pulled out a sack of 200G (Alexa took 7 damage trying to get it)
	- The mess hall: nothing of interest
	- The galley: nothing of value
- Drok goes and grabs the crowbar
- Meanwhile, Norton uses Identify to determine what's going on with the rusty iron helmet, the boots, and the wax paper wrapped packet.
	- The helmet is mundane, Norton tosses it overboard
	- The boots are Boots of Elvenkind
	- The packet contains a logbook from a member of the crew of the Compass Rose. It details a prayer by Alethea, the previous captain of the ship to the god of the undead. She made it over the braided hair talisman: she wants to be united with her husband no matter their end. The god of the dead seems to have granted her prayer - she and the rest of her crew are coming back as zombies.
- The party makes it back to Dragon's Rest. Renara, Rix, Laylee, Varnath, etc. are praying in the temple. There's a statue in the middle of a man with his arms out with canaries sitting on them.
- The party joins in the prayer time. A feeling of serenity washes over us.
- Norton explains what we found on the Compass Rose. Alethea's husband, Brastos, died 80 years ago and is buried atop the hill on the island near the shipwreck.
- Everyone seems happy with us. We take our leave.

We are now level 2!
