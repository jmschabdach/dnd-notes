**Onward**

These fires were started when Wigglewuggle escaped with [[Sir Talavar]], according to Duke Ickrinds. He gives [[Bix]] a brooch to wear with meeting the king.

We proceed past the building to another bridge. There's a guy on the bridge talking about how [[Agdon Longscarf]] stole his head. He's a scarecrow and currently has a gourd for a head. He's talking to a bunch of bullywug heads on pikes. They're former kings.

Bix talks to them and finds out that the former kings aren't fans of the current king. He also talks to the scarecrow, finds out his name is [[Clapperclaw]]. If we get him is original head back (a deer skull), he can show us the way to [[Tither]].

We continue to the next island and meet the king. He asks us to return a book to [[Bavlorna]] on his behalf and tells us we have to join his court first. Mara identifies that we don't but we kinda go with it.

Once we suit up, Bix talks to the king.
- Why did we have to meet you first? 
    - That's just how it is, the Soggy Court protects the good bullywugs from outsiders
- Tell us about your glorious rise to power
    - He led a coup against the former king
    - He's proud of the betrayal
    - It happened 3 weeks ago

We head out of the court. Glurk gets passed a note:
> "Find Illig The Baron of Muckstump at once. The Revolution lives!"

We try to go get a breath of fresh air behind the court. We are warned away from the Proving Grounds that are used in Trials by Combat, and away from the holding cells.

Mara tries to get more information about this baron of muckstump and accidentally talks the court into giving us titles
- Duke Glurk of This and That
- Supreme Leader Bix
- Mara the Flamefighter

Bix examines the book. It's basically a book of strong dislike (not magical) with a list of who [[Bavlorna]] dislikes and why. The hag [[Scabatha Nightshade]] is in it.
- [[Clapperclaw]] was made by [[Skabatha Nightshade]] using the soul of a tortured child

Glurk asks and finds out that there's a shop in a Rain Balloon behind the Holding Cell run by Trinket and Bauble.

We go to the Holding Cell and meet [[Margot]]. She was an accomplice of [[Sir Talavar]] and Wigglewog's. She regards [[Bavlorna]] as a bitter enemy.
- We tell her that [[Sir Talavar]] has escaped and Wigglewog is dead
- She doesn't know of [[Baron of Muckstump]] but suggests we talk to [[Sir Voolp]], who is another member of the [[Soggy Court]]
- She gives us enough of a description of him that we could recognize him.
- She tells us she'll probably be faced with trial by combat. We'll try to break her out later at night.

The Rain Balloon of "Wonderous Wares and Fair Fares" run by Trinket and Bauble, a pair of darklings. Trinket offers an item in exchange for the color of your iris, Bauble offers an item in exchange for the beat in your step.
- Items for sale
    - Very good thimble (fingertip not included)
    - Mug of Bumble Beer (has a nice sting to it)
    - Duskmallow pie (with decorative bite marks)
    - Bundle of dry wood (great for starting fires)
    - Ink portraits (if we have to look at you, so should you)
    - Moonlight monocle (no more fumbling in the dark) 
    - Unicorn horn
- Mara gives Trinket the color of her eyes (purple) in exchange for the unicorn horn. Her irises are now translucent. Is this the unicorn horn mentioned in all the riddles?

End of session side quest summary
- "Deliver the book to Bavlorna"
- Free [[Margot]]
- Find the [[Baron of Muckstump]] (Illig), possibly with the help of [[Sir Voolp]]
