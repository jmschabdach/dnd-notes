### Summary

Mara, Bix, and Glurk meet at the entrance to the Witchlight Carnival where they are given tickets. Their tickets allow them in and give them 8 punches to use at various attractions around the carnival. They shared buttons with the monkey working the Calliope, attempted to ride dragonflies through an aerial obstacle course, watch a water show at Silversong Lake, struggle but win at Topple Tower, get their fortunes told, and attend the Bigtop Extravaganza. On the way, they offer to help look for a lost displacer beast kit, try to chase down a human male who spooked a dragonfly, and enjoy some snacks.
