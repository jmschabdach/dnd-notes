### Summary

We accidentally killed Elmer when he tried to attack [[Bix]], but were able to get [[Jingle Jangle]]'s key and [[Clapperclaw]]'s head. We returned them both to their owners: [[Jingle Jangle]] let us keep the key and [[Clapperclaw]] offered to take us to [[Tither]].

On the way to do errands for [[Bavlorna]] we meet Octavia, an elf who lost her heart in a deal with the hag. Back at [[Bavlorna]]'s hut, [[Mara]] did some exploring and accidentally found the taxidermy workshop where the hag was waiting for the delivery. In exchange for the crate, she gave [[Bix]] his sense of direction back.

[[Mara]] continued snooping and found a secret password (spittlespew) maybe? Could it lead inside the magic cauldron? Meanwhile [[Glurk]] stuck his limb into the well and angered the Gelatinous Cylinder hiding inside. After it left the well, the pool began to fill with regular water.
