### Previously
We messed around with an Oh-Well then walked for a bit. We eventually crossed paths with [[The Inn at the End of the Road]] and went in. After chatting with the innkeeper, a human druid named [[Sue Harabax]], we had dinner and spent the night. The next morning the Inn dropped us off at the edge of the [[Tollway]], where we got our butts thoroughly kicked by the [[Rabbit Mafia]]. As we fled, we did catch sight of [[Agdon Longscarf]], the brigand prince of Prismeer. Wandering through the swamp in the direction [[Mara]] believes is toward [[The Watcher]], we had an encounter with mud mephits. They let us pass when we were able to solve the riddle of their favorite food (mud pies).
