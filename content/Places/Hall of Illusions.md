An attraction in the [[Witchlight Carnival]]. [[Candlefoot]] takes tickets at the door. Don't go in by yourself - you might not come back out. 

Upon entering, you are surrounded by mirrors. The ones nearest the entrance show the viewer in their youth. As you go through the halls, your reflection ages.

###### [[C02 Session 01]]
- The party learns from displacer beast [[Dirla]] that her child [[Star]] disappeared from the Carnival 8 years ago.
- According to Auntie Edna the fortune teller, "A child was lured away by a pig 8 years ago." Could this be [[Star]]?

###### [[C02 Session 02]]
- The party watches a halfling propose to another halfling out front. The proposee, Ween, stands in range of a statue of Tasha (of the Hideous Laughter) and promptly starts laughing. This upsets the proposer, Reuben, who runs off into the Hall by himself.
- The party enters the Hall with Ween to try to find Reuben. [[Mara]] and Ween have no luck, but [[Glurk]] and [[Bix]] watch [[Girl in a Pig Mask]] suck Reuben into a mirrors.
