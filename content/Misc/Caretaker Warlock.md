## Class features

**Proficiencies**
Skills: removed Deception and Intimidation and replaced them with Handle Animal and Insight.

**Patron Insignis** (replaces Otherworldly Patron)
You are in possession of your patron. Whether it is a hatchling or an egg is up to you and your Dungeon Master

**Entrusted Invocations** (replaces Eldritch Invocations)
See pages 30-31 of the handbook

**Entrusted Boon** (replaces Pact Boon)
Available boons
- **Entrusted Awareness**: new boon, grants enhanced perception and vision to caretaker and allies
- **Entrusted Servant** (replaces Pact of the Chain): The patron's presence attracts a follower. No mechanical changes
- **Entrusted Shield**: New boon functioning similarly to Pact Blade/Entrusted Blade but more defensive

## Class

**Hit Points**
- Hit dice: 1d8 per warlock level
- Hit points at first level: 8 + Constitution modifier
- Hit points at higher levels: 1d8 (or 5) + Constitution modifier per warlock level after 1st

**Proficiencies**
- Armor: Light armor
- Weapons: simple weapons
- Tools: none
- Saving throws: Wisdom, Charisma
- Skills: Choose 2 from *Arcana, Handle Animal, History, Insight, Investigation, Nature,* and *Religion*

**Equipment** (in addition to the equipment granted by background)
- (a) light crossbow and 20 bolts or (b) any simple weapon
- (a) a component pouch or (b) an arcane focus
- (a) a scholar's pack or (b) a dungeoneer's pack
- Leather armor, any simple weapon, and 2 daggers

**Patron Insignis**
- 1st level: you are in possession of an egg or baby patron
- Features granted at 1st, 6th, 10th, and 14th levels

**Imparted Spells**
- *Cantrips*: 2 cantrips of your choice from the warlock spell list
- *Spell Slots*: see table on page 32; all spell slots are restored at the end of short and long rests
- Spells Known (1st level or higher)
    - 1st level: 2 1st level spells of your choice from the warlock spell table
    - When you gain a new level in this class, you may choose one of the warlock spells you know and replace it with another spell from the warlock spell list (at a level for which you have spell slots)
- Spellcasting Ability
    - Charisma
    - Spell Save DC: 8 + proficiency bonus + Charisma modifier
    - Spell attack modifier: proficiency bonus + Charisma modifier
- Focus: either your patron or an arcane focus

**Bestowed Invocations**
- At 2nd level, gain 2 bestowed invocations of choice
- Can swap 1 invocation for a different one when gaining a level in this class
- Invocation requirements must be met at the time you learn the invocation

**Entrusted Boon**
At 3rd level, your patron bestows a gift on you for your loyal service
- Entrusted Awareness: if you are within 60 feet of your patron,  you gain Darkvision and a Perception bonus equal to your Charisma modifier
- Entrusted Blade: summon a magical weapon. The weapon is treated as magical for resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage. You can spend 1 hour performing a ritual on a magic weapon to apply the bestowed weapon to it.
- Entrusted Servant (replaces Pact of the Chain): 
    - Gain a familiar and the Find Familiar spell (does not count against number of spells known, can be performed as a ritual)
    - When you attack, you can forgo one of your attacks to allow your familiar to make one attack with its reaction
    - Familiar may also care for egg
- Entrusted Shield: Summon a shield in your empty hand. You are proficient with it, it counts as magical, and you gain +2 on Con saves. Otherwise behaves like Entrusted Blade
- Entrusted Tome: Choose 3 cantrips from any class's spell list. While the book is on your person, you can cast those cantrips at will. They don't count against your number of cantrips known. If you lose your grimoire, you can perform a 1-hour ceremony to receive a replacement from your patron (short or long rest).

**Ability score improvement**: 4th, 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th levels

**Mystic Arcanum**
Your patron eventually bestows on you a magic secret called an arcanum. You gain a Xth level spell from the warlock list. You may cast this spell once without expending a spell slot. You must finish a long rest before doing so again. All uses of Mystic Arcanum recharge when you finish a long rest.
- At level 11, gain a 6th level spell
- At level 13, gain a 7th level spell
- At level 15, gain an 8th level spell
- At level 17, gain a 9th level spells

**Parting gift** (replaces Eldritch Master)
Only matters when your patron hatches or is powerful enough to ascend on its own
